2. PURPOSE AND NATURE OF SERVICE
---------------------------------
Tarjuma is an ad-free, spiritually focused Islamic audio application designed to 
facilitate listening, understanding, and reflecting upon the Holy Quran. 

The App offers:
- High-quality Arabic recitations from world-renowned scholars and reciters.
- Urdu and Hindi audio translations (translation by Fateh Muhammed Jalandhary).
- Five distinctive listening modes (Surah-wise Arabic, Para-wise Arabic & Translation, 
  Para-wise Translation Only, Surah-wise Arabic & Translation, Surah-wise Translation Only).
- Curated and personalized mixes ("Made For You").
- Ambient natural background sounds (Rain, Birds, Waves, Crickets, Fire, Owl) for focus and meditation.
- Offline audio downloads for listening without an internet connection.
- Listening streak and daily goal tracking.
- Custom user playlists and bookmarks.
- Anonymous cloud synchronization via a unique Sync Key.
